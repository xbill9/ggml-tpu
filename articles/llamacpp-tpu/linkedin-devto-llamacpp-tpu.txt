llama.cpp on a Cloud TPU: An Unofficial ggml Backend Through XLA

ggml-tpu lowers each llama.cpp graph to one StableHLO program, compiles it with XLA and runs it on a TPU through libtpu. Gemma 4 E2B on one TPU v5e chip: build, test every operation, serve, chat, speculative decoding, and a three-cell Colab notebook.

What is in it:

- 🟢 122.56 tokens per second decode, 7641.86 tokens per second prompt
- 🟢 129.42 to 151.67 tokens per second with the exact Q4_0 QAT drafter
- 🟢 Every operation tested matches the CPU on the TPU, SUM 7/8 aside
- 🟢 Partial offload works at -ngl 10, -ngl 25 and -ngl 99
